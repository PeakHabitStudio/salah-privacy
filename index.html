Salah — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Peak Habit Studio. Last updated: 2 October 2026.

This notice covers Salah — Prayer Times & Qibla for Android phones, tablets and TV (com.salahprayer.multiplatform), Salah for Wear OS (com.salahprayer.wear), and Salah for Windows.

Data used on your device

Salah calculates prayer times and the Qibla bearing on your device. If you grant location permission, it uses coordinates provided by your operating system. You can instead enter a manual location. Coordinates, location labels, language, calculation preferences and reminder settings are stored locally to provide these features. Compass readings are used locally for direction guidance. The optional Guard timer does not verify or report whether you prayed.

Data sent to the publisher

The app has no account system, advertising SDK or analytics service. It does not send your coordinates, preferences, compass readings or prayer activity to Peak Habit Studio. Prayer calculations and reminders do not require a publisher server.

Permissions and controls

Location permission is optional. Notification permission enables local prayer reminders. You can change the location manually, disable reminders in Settings, and revoke permissions in your device settings. Removing the app or clearing its app data removes the local device copy according to your platform's behavior.

Platform services and backups

Your operating system's location services, app store, backups and device transfers are controlled by their providers and your platform settings. Android currently permits system backup of app data; that backup may contain your locally saved settings. Salah does not run its own backup or synchronization service.

This public page and support

This public notice is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it online sends ordinary web requests to GitHub, which handles technical request information under its privacy statement. An offline copy is available in the Android app's Settings.

If you email support, Peak Habit Studio receives the email address and information you choose to include, and uses that correspondence to respond to your enquiry. You can request deletion of that correspondence, subject to required record retention.
